## 🚀 P2A: Founding Tester Beta (14 days, max 25 testers)

Reflexhon is running a short, invite-only public beta learning program: 14 days, capped at 25 founding testers. It's a chance to test the Papiamentu cultural-AI chat and dataset explorer, and to help correct it before a wider release.

- **Sign up:** [Founding tester interest form](https://forms.gle/G1iYxEPqV2Zpd6447)
- **What to expect and how to give feedback:** see [docs/beta/FOUNDING_TESTER_GUIDE.md](docs/beta/FOUNDING_TESTER_GUIDE.md)
- **Program plan:** see [docs/beta/P2A_LAUNCH_PLAN.md](docs/beta/P2A_LAUNCH_PLAN.md)

Joining this beta creates no financial entitlement — no payment, equity, tokens, or points. It is a product-learning program, not an incentive or rewards program.

---

## 🪙 Future governance and token ideas (not part of this beta)

Community-governance and token concepts (including the working names "ReflexCoin" and "ReflexPoints") have come up as long-term ideas in earlier planning notes. They are **not implemented, not offered, and not promised** in this project today. Any future decision to build them would require separate legal and governance review before anything is announced. Until and unless that happens, they create no financial entitlement, no transferable value, and no launch timeline, and they are entirely separate from the P2A public beta described above.
